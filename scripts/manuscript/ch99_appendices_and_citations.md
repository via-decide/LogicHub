# Appendix A: Citations and Open Source Credits

The Daxini Stack and the LogicHub engine are built upon the knowledge freely shared by the global open-source community, electronics manufacturers, and academic researchers. 

## Primary References
- **Daxini BCA Learning Repository**: Ground zero for the "Learning First" philosophy, architecting systems from the database up.
- **via-decide Electronics Knowledge Base**: The foundational documentation for design-for-evidence, timing constraints, RTOS architectures, and validation procedures.
- **LogicHub Core**: The deterministic engine designed to perfectly serialize hardware graphs into the physical 8 MB storage cartridge.

## Component Specifications and Datasheets
The strict rules evaluated by LogicHub rely entirely on officially published datasheets (like the Winbond W25Q64JV 64 Mbit SPI NOR flash), thermal derating curves, and physical properties specified by major manufacturers. No estimates are treated as verified facts.

## Typography and Tooling
- **Pandoc / EPUB3**: This distribution relies on the EPUB3 standard for robust, offline-capable digital reading.
- **Markdown**: The universal language of our documentation layer.
