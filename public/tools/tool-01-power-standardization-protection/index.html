Universal Tools / T01

Power Standardization & Protection

Design pattern for hardware that “just works” when powered: protected inputs, known rails, current budget, safe brownout behaviour, and clear production gates before any user or supplier touches the device.

Contract Definition

LayerMeaning
SOURCEUSB-C receptacle, DC jack, battery pack, bench PSU, EVSE supply, solar/battery system, robot pack, machine mains input.
ENTRYConnector, fuse/PTC, reverse-protection MOSFET/diode, TVS, ESD, EMI filter, ideal diode, power switch.
INTENTPower the system safely while rejecting wrong polarity, overcurrent, overvoltage, ESD, brownout, and unsafe plug/unplug states.
STATE/LOGICRails good, rails bad, brownout, overload, thermal throttle, battery low, charger connected, USB-only, external PSU, service mode.
ACTIONSEnable regulator, latch fault, reset MCU, cut motor/heater relay, show status, log event, block write/commit, enter safe mode.
OUTPUT/METRICSMeasured rail voltage, ripple, current, temperature, inrush, reset count, runtime, fuse trip margin, pass/fail receipt.

Production Stages

Stage 0
Input contract freeze.Define exact power sources: USB-C 5V only, USB-C PD, battery, 12V/24V DC, mains-derived PSU, PoE, solar/battery, bench-only.
Stage 1
Protection topology.Choose fuse/PTC, reverse-polarity method, TVS/ESD, connector rating, switch, ideal diode, soft-start/inrush control.
Stage 2
Rail architecture.Map all rails, regulator efficiency, dropout, sequencing, always-on rail, switched rail, analog/digital/noisy-motor separation.
Stage 3
Load budget.List every load with idle, typical, peak, startup/inrush, stall, heater max, RF peak, camera peak, motor transient.
Stage 4
Brownout and fault policy.Define what shuts down first, what logs, what remains powered, and what the user sees.
Stage 5
Layout and wiring release.Validate trace width, copper, connector current rating, creepage/clearance where relevant, star ground, motor/heater separation.
Stage 6
First-power procedure.Power with current-limited bench supply, no expensive modules inserted, record rail voltages and thermal rise.
Stage 7
Evidence receipt.Save measured voltages, currents, temperatures, pass/fail gates, photos, instrument IDs, operator, date, revision.
